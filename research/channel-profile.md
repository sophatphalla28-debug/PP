## Audience
- Korean, skews **45+** (retirement / wealth-preservation mindset), aspirational middle class.
- Cares about: how much is "enough," reaching financial security, not wasting money, retirement, catching up late, protecting what they have.
- Motivated by **milestones, self-comparison, and fear of falling behind** more than by macro/policy news.

## Successful-topic patterns (proven on THIS channel)
- Concrete **won-amount milestones** framed as "what silently changes" → 3억의 법칙 (150K), 4억 넘기면 (12K), 부자들이 절대 사지 않는 7가지 (19K).
- **50s + "no money" threshold fear** → 1억 없이 50대 (13K), 노후에 돈 없는 사람 (38K).
- **Counterintuitive hooks** → 열심히 일할수록 가난 (13K).
- Titles with a hidden secret / "아무도 알려주지 않은" / "조용히 시작되는 변화."

## Weak-topic patterns (proven weak on THIS channel)
- **Pure macro/economics explainers** → 인플레이션이 당신의 돈을 갉아먹는 법 (1.3K), 카드회사는 어떻게 돈을 버는가 (1K). The audience does not reward abstract macro.
- **Over-repeated milestone videos** — the milestone format is saturating: newest milestone uploads now do 1.5–4K vs. the 30–150K of the earlier hits. Diminishing returns.
- Generic habit videos with no new hook → 습관 영상을 아무리 봐도 (2K).

## Strategic takeaway
The channel wins on **evergreen wealth-psychology + concrete personal thresholds**, NOT on macro news. Best new topics = the channel's proven emotional formats (milestone / counterintuitive / 50s-fear) applied to **under-covered retirement-income & tax mechanics that carry a real won-figure consequence**, with a timely 2026 hook where one exists.
